species_names.tsv

English names come from our own extraction of Pokemon Platinum (English, CPUE).

Japanese, French, Italian, German, Spanish and Korean names come from PokeAPI,
data/v2/csv/pokemon_species_names.csv at commit
6ea97b5641faa12e090866e8144ae8f57764fc64 (https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi),
fetched 2026-11-08. They are stored the way Gen 4 games store them: uppercase,
except Japanese and Korean. Every name was checked against PKHeX's Gen 4 names,
and the German names also against German HeartGold (IPKD). Where they differ,
the table holds the Gen 4 name:

- French: Gen 4 uppercase names have no accents, so 75 names lose them
  (PokeAPI "Salamèche", Gen 4 "SALAMECHE"). Species: 4, 35, 36, 49, 56, 61, 69, 81, 82, 101, 112, 117, 118, 123, 125, 130, 132, 133, 142, 145, 154, 155, 173, 192, 198, 200, 202, 223, 226, 229, 239, 241, 257, 261, 262, 264, 270, 277, 278, 289, 302, 307, 310, 312, 315, 323, 330, 336, 337, 341, 351, 355, 356, 358, 360, 375, 376, 395, 396, 397, 398, 402, 418, 419, 429, 436, 437, 438, 456, 457, 462, 466, 480, 481, 482.
- Italian 83: PokeAPI "Farfetch’d" with a curly apostrophe, Gen 4 "FARFETCH'D".
- Spanish 83: PokeAPI "Farfetch’d" with a curly apostrophe, Gen 4 "FARFETCH'D".
