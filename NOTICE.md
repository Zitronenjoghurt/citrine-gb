### Projects Citrine's code follows

Parts of Citrine are translated from these projects or follow how they work:

- melonDS by the melonDS team, GPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/melonDS-emu/melonDS: the DS 3D renderer and geometry engine, DMA and ARM7 bus timings, direct boot, wifi calibration data, wifi timers and registers, the sound channels, capture and mixer, and the Action Replay DS cheat engine
- AltWFC, AGPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/barronwaffles/dwc_network_server_emulator: the replies Citrine gives in place of the DS online services. These parts stay under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later
- NanoBoyAdvance by fleroviux, GPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/nba-emu/NanoBoyAdvance: when a GBA timer starts counting
- higan by Near, GPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/higan-emu/higan: the GBA colour correction
- PKHeX by Kaphotics, GPL-3.0, https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX: the Gen 4 text table
- pokeplatinum by pret, https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum: 41 characters of the Gen 4 text table
- blip_buf by Shay Green, LGPL-2.1-or-later: the sound buffer, through the Rust port by Mathijs van de Nes under Libraries
- Poké Classic Network by Megan Edwards, https://github.com/mm201/pkmn-classic-framework: the relay server's GTS request handling
